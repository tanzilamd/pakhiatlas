## Name and identification

A familiar starling-family bird, distinct from the hill myna. Similar common names do not mean the same species or the same care requirements.

## Behaviour and needs

Watch natural ground foraging and social behaviour without disturbing nests. Human food scraps are not a care plan for a rescued bird. Obtain qualified wildlife help if a bird is injured or unable to fend for itself.
