## Name and identification

A dark glossy starling-family bird with conspicuous bare skin around the head. It is different from the common myna. The ability to mimic sounds varies; “talking myna” is not a guarantee or an adequate identity.

## Behaviour and needs

Mynas are not seed-eating parrots. A species-appropriate softbill diet and veterinary attention to iron intake are important; rice, fruit alone or an arbitrary parrot mix is not a complete feeding plan. Do not set supplement doses at home.
