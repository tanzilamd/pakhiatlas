## Name and identification

A long-tailed parakeet. Adult male neck markings can be helpful, but juveniles, females and colour mutations complicate identification. “Yellow parrot” may describe a lutino bird; it is not a scientific species name.

## Behaviour and needs

Ringneck calls and flight needs should not be underestimated. A small decorative cage does not meet the needs of an active long-tailed bird. Appearance does not prove captive origin, and talking is not guaranteed.
