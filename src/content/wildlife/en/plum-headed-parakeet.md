## Name and identification

A distinct parakeet species, not a red-headed colour mutation of an Indian ringneck. Head colour differs with age and sex, so a single adult photograph is not a universal identification key.

## Behaviour and needs

Observe social and flight behaviour at a respectful distance. Do not disturb nest sites or take a wild bird home because a similar bird is sold as a pet.
