## Meet the bird

A rosella with a red head, white cheek patches, yellow breast and green-blue areas. Several bright colours occur in the normal plumage. Natural range: Southeastern Australia and Tasmania.

## Recognising the species

White cheeks help identification. Ask for a full species identity rather than relying on the broad seller label “rosella”.

## Behaviour and companionship

Flight and observational keeping often suit this bird better than frequent handling. Do not assume safe mixing with smaller birds.

## Housing, movement and cleanup

Provide spacious flight, shelter and separated feeding positions. Be ready to separate birds if chasing, cornering or food exclusion occurs.

## Planning its diet

Choose appropriate feed and look beneath seed husks to check food availability. Introduce vegetables patiently without withholding food to force acceptance.

## Pairing and breeding welfare

Behaviour can change around pairing and nesting. Hanging a nest in a crowded mixed cage is not a sound breeding plan.

## Before bringing one home

Choose an independently feeding bird with documented identity and responsible provenance. Keep new birds separate and discuss an initial examination with a veterinarian. Persistent fluffed posture, breathing difficulty or stopping eating need prompt assessment. Do not take an unweaned chick and start inexperienced hand-feeding.
