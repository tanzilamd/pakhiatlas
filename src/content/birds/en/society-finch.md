## Identity and varieties

A domestic finch lineage commonly called Bengalese or society finch. Its domestic form should not be presented as a newly discovered species. Colour and crest varieties belong under the same entry.

Natural ancestry: Domesticated lineage; not a separate wild species.

## Living space and companionship

Prioritise horizontal flight space, compatible companionship and several places to feed. These are observation birds; repeated catching to “tame” them can cause unnecessary stress. Keep the enclosure away from larger hook-billed birds.
