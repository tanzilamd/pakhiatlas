## Identity and varieties

A compact African parrot. A smaller body than an African grey does not mean a weak bite or low need for enrichment. Individual tolerance of handling varies.

Natural ancestry: West Africa.

## Living space and companionship

Build trust with several household members gradually and respect body language. Give independent foraging tasks, safe wood and supervised exercise. Avoid forcing contact when the bird retreats or guards a space.
