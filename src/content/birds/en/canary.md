## Identity and varieties

A domestic songbird with selected song, colour and form varieties. Singing is variable; neither sex nor health should be diagnosed from a song alone. A breed name is not a separate scientific species.

Natural ancestry: Domestic lineage from Atlantic island canaries.

## Living space and companionship

Provide flight room and a predictable light-and-rest routine. Do not assume another canary is automatically welcome: watch territorial behavior, especially around breeding. Avoid prolonging light exposure just to obtain more song or eggs.

Consider adult condition, seasonal state and compatibility, not just age. Do not breed through an active molt or a health problem. Keep a separation option for incompatible birds and plan carefully before introducing a nest.
