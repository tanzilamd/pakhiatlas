## Meet the bird

A large white cockatoo with a broad white crest that opens like an umbrella. It differs from the yellow-crested sulphur-crested cockatoo. Natural range: The northern Maluku islands of Indonesia.

## Recognising the species

A raised crest is not simply a sign of happiness; it can accompany arousal or distress. An affectionate appearance does not remove loud calls, chewing or powder down.

## Behaviour and companionship

Even a sociable bird needs a sustainable routine. Build independent play, foraging and calm contact with more than one caregiver instead of constant cuddling.

## Housing, movement and cleanup

Housing, locks and chewable materials must withstand a powerful beak. Manage dust and consider household respiratory sensitivity before acquisition.

## Planning its diet

Combine a balanced cockatoo diet with suitable vegetables and foraging. Nuts or sunflower seeds should not dominate daily feeding.

## Pairing and breeding welfare

Body stroking, nest-like cavities and mate-like dependency can influence reproductive behaviour. Seek veterinary advice for laying or persistent behaviour problems.
