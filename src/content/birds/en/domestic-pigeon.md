## Identity and varieties

Giribaz, fantail and tumbler describe domestic breeds or types, not separate scientific species. A domestic pigeon is different from treating every wild dove as a pet.

Natural ancestry: Domestic lineage of the rock dove.

## Living space and companionship

Provide a dry, ventilated loft with secure protection from predators, enough landing and walking space, and clean feed and water. Prevent damp accumulated droppings. Manage flock density and keep newcomers separate initially.

## Pairing, nesting and parent welfare

[Before the nest: pairing and breeding readiness](../../guides/breeding-readiness/)

Both parents take part in rearing young; separating a parent or moving a nest can disrupt care. Use actual age records rather than size or cooing as a readiness test. Do not allow constant nesting at the expense of parental condition.
