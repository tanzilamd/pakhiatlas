## Identification

A specialised nectar-feeding parrot. Its colourful appearance must not distract from feeding and hygiene needs that differ from ordinary seed-based parrot assumptions.

Natural range: Australia. Ask for accurate identity, hatch history, current food and care records rather than relying on colour or a market name.

## Housing and daily activity

Arrange suitable flight, bathing and washable surrounding surfaces. A nectar-based feeding plan requires careful preparation, clean containers and prompt removal of spoiled food. Follow the chosen species-appropriate product and veterinary guidance rather than inventing a sugar-water recipe.

[Housing & a bird-safe home](../../guides/safe-home/)

## Feeding and water

Follow a suitable specialist lorikeet diet and careful food hygiene; seed, fruit alone or improvised sugar water is not a complete ration.

[A practical daily bird-care routine](../../guides/daily-routine/)

## Droppings, odour and cleaning

Plan for wet droppings and food splashes associated with a nectar-based diet. Choose washable surfaces and remove moist food and waste promptly.

[Understanding bird droppings](../../guides/droppings/)

## Before planned breeding

[Before the nest: pairing and breeding readiness](../../guides/breeding-readiness/)

Weakness, straining, sitting low or breathing difficulty need prompt professional assessment. Do not manipulate eggs or attempt amateur syringe feeding of unweaned chicks.
