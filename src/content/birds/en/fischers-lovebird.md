## Identity and varieties

A white eye-ring lovebird, distinct from peach-faced and masked lovebirds. Colour mutations can obscure familiar markings; identify parentage before naming a variety.

Natural ancestry: East Africa.

## Living space and companionship

Provide plenty of chewing and foraging opportunities, with safe fittings a strong little beak cannot dismantle. Do not assume a pair tolerates a third lovebird. Monitor whether both birds can reach food and rest.

## Pairing, nesting and parent welfare

[Before the nest: pairing and breeding readiness](../../guides/breeding-readiness/)

The sexes can look alike. Do not rely on pelvic feeling, head shape or a seller’s guess. Establish identity and unrelated parentage before considering breeding; do not mix lovebird species to produce saleable colour names.
