## Identification

A large white cockatoo with a yellow crest. A yellow crest does not make it the same bird as a wholly yellow lutino parakeet; “yellow parrot” is an ambiguous market name.

Natural range: Australia and New Guinea region. Ask for accurate identity, hatch history, current food and care records rather than relying on colour or a market name.

## Housing and daily activity

Plan for strong chewing, loud calls, feather dust and substantial daily activity. Secure fittings and safe independent enrichment matter. Do not create constant body contact that the household cannot sustain; use gradual, voluntary interaction and rest.

[Housing & a bird-safe home](../../guides/safe-home/)

## Feeding and water

A favourite-seed ration can be incomplete. Introduce appropriate balanced foods gradually and watch actual consumption; never starve a bird into accepting a new food.

[A practical daily bird-care routine](../../guides/daily-routine/)

## Droppings, odour and cleaning

Favourite perches outside the enclosure also collect droppings. Plan washable protection and remove spoiled food, damp lining and accumulated waste rather than masking odour.

Weakness, straining, sitting low or breathing difficulty need prompt professional assessment. Do not manipulate eggs or attempt amateur syringe feeding of unweaned chicks.
