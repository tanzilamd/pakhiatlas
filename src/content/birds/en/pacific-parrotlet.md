## Identity and varieties

A very small parrot with substantial behavioural needs. Small size does not make it safe to mix with other species or handle roughly.

Natural ancestry: Western South America.

## Living space and companionship

Check bar spacing, tiny gaps and toy fittings carefully. Offer foraging challenges scaled to the beak and feet. Protect the bird from larger companions; also watch for territorial behaviour toward smaller birds.
