## Identity and varieties

A distinct conure species, not simply a colour mutation of the sun conure. Young birds and hybrids complicate visual identification; request accurate records.

Natural ancestry: Brazil.

## Living space and companionship

Plan around penetrating contact calls and an active beak. Provide daily supervised exercise, foraging and appropriate chew materials. A cage mate is not a replacement for individual observation and a safe routine.

## Pairing, nesting and parent welfare

[Before the nest: pairing and breeding readiness](../../guides/breeding-readiness/)

Confirm species identity and unrelated parentage before pairing. Do not breed with sun conures merely for a hybrid sales label. Nest defence can alter handling tolerance; avoid coercion and keep a separation plan.
