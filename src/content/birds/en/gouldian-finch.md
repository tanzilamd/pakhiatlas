## Identity and varieties

An Australian finch also encountered under the older genus name Erythrura. Head colours and captive mutations are variations within the species, not separate species.

Natural ancestry: Northern Australia.

## Living space and companionship

Keep routines and conditions stable, with protected rest, suitable ventilation and clean dry feeding areas. Avoid crowding and abrupt changes during molt. Do not invent one “tropical” temperature setting for every home.

Bright adult plumage is not a complete readiness test. Avoid breeding a bird under molt, with poor body condition or an unexplained health change. Do not assume routine fostering is necessary or harmless.
