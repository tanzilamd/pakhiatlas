## Identity and varieties

Also called the black-hooded parakeet; older references may use Nandayus nenday. A black head is a clue, not enough to identify every bird or hybrid.

Natural ancestry: Central South America.

## Living space and companionship

Consider the household and neighbours before acquiring a loud social conure. Offer vigorous safe activity and destructible enrichment. Do not attempt to eliminate normal contact calls through punishment or constant cage covering.
