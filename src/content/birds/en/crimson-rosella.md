## Meet the bird

Typical adults show deep red plumage, blue cheek and wing areas and dark back patterning. Young birds can be much greener. Natural range: Forested habitats in eastern and southeastern Australia.

## Recognising the species

Compare the usual blue cheek with the white cheek of the eastern rosella. Age and subspecies can alter appearance.

## Behaviour and companionship

Think of a flight-active aviary parrot rather than a guaranteed cuddly hand companion. Watch aggression during introductions.

## Housing, movement and cleanup

Provide long flight paths and sturdy perches. Do not keep small birds or competing pairs together when territorial behaviour develops.

## Planning its diet

Use a balanced grass-parrot diet with suitable plant-food variety. Do not let preferences for fruit or sunflower decide the whole ration.

## Pairing and breeding welfare

Nesting can increase territoriality. A breeding plan needs separate space where necessary, observation and safe chick care arrangements.
