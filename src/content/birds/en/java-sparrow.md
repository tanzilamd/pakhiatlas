## Identity and varieties

A large, robust finch also listed as Lonchura oryzivora in some references. Domestic white and other colour forms do not establish a separate species or proof of lawful provenance.

Natural ancestry: Java and nearby Indonesian islands.

## Living space and companionship

Allow useful flight space and watch interactions with smaller finches. Multiple food access points help reveal whether a dominant bird is excluding another. Do not feed only rice simply because it is called a rice bird.
