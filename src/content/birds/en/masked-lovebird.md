## Identity and varieties

Also called the yellow-collared lovebird. The wild-type dark mask is a useful visual clue, but colour alone is not sufficient to identify mutations or hybrids.

Natural ancestry: East Africa.

## Living space and companionship

Plan housing around individual compatibility, not the word “lovebird”. Territorial birds may exclude a cage mate. Provide separate feeding access and a way to separate birds safely. Keep small finches away from the beak.
