## Identity and varieties

A small Australian dove, distinct from Bangladesh’s native wild doves. The name “dove” does not mean all species can be housed or treated in the same way.

Natural ancestry: Australia.

## Living space and companionship

Offer both flight room and a clean, dry floor area for walking and feeding. Avoid wire flooring that is unsuitable for the feet. These delicate birds should not share an enclosure with aggressive parrots.

Look for an actually compatible pair rather than relying on eye-ring appearance alone. Keep nest checks unobtrusive and watch whether parents continue normal feeding. Do not encourage repeated clutches simply because nests are small.
