## Identity and varieties

A social aviary finch. Australian and Sunda zebra finches have differing modern taxonomic treatments; verify origin and scientific identity before publishing a more specific label. Domestic colour mutations are not new species.

Natural ancestry: Australia and nearby islands; taxonomic treatment varies.

## Living space and companionship

Prioritise horizontal flight and compatible finch company over handling. Offer more than one feeding point and observe bullying. Frequent catching to “bond” with a finch can undermine welfare; watch its natural behavior instead.

## Pairing, nesting and parent welfare

[Before the nest: pairing and breeding readiness](../../guides/breeding-readiness/)

Finches may show reproductive behavior young; this is not a breeding recommendation. Do not leave nest sites available merely to keep a group occupied. Record parentage and prevent overcrowding from repeated unplanned clutches.
