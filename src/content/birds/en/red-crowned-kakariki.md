## Meet the bird

An active green parakeet with a red crown. Yellow-crowned kakariki are a different species, not just another colour label. Natural range: New Zealand and associated islands.

## Recognising the species

It explores floors and cover as well as flying. Check its location before opening doors because a fast-moving bird can escape unnoticed.

## Behaviour and companionship

Do not restrain normal activity merely to keep it in your hand. Offer foraging and let the bird approach at its own pace.

## Housing, movement and cleanup

Provide long flight paths, a safe floor, clean water and dry feeding stations. Do not leave food mixed with droppings or damp substrate.

## Planning its diet

Use a balanced small-parakeet diet. Its exploration can be supported with suitable food in clean foraging trays.

## Pairing and breeding welfare

Pairing and nesting can increase territorial behaviour. Quarantine and observe new birds separately; a nest box does not establish breeding readiness.

## Before bringing one home

Choose an independently feeding bird with documented identity and responsible provenance. Keep new birds separate and discuss an initial examination with a veterinarian. Persistent fluffed posture, breathing difficulty or stopping eating need prompt assessment. Do not take an unweaned chick and start inexperienced hand-feeding.
