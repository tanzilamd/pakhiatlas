## Identity and varieties

A distinct, brightly coloured conure, not interchangeable with the Jenday. Juvenile colour and hybrids make identification more complicated than comparing one adult photograph.

Natural ancestry: Northeastern South America.

## Living space and companionship

Plan for loud contact calls before acquisition. Sound tolerance, neighbours and reliable daily interaction matter. Give safe flight, foraging and destructible enrichment; do not punish normal vocalisation or expect talking on demand.

## Pairing, nesting and parent welfare

[Before the nest: pairing and breeding readiness](../../guides/breeding-readiness/)

Obtain known age and ancestry rather than pairing only for colour. Keep separate secure housing available if compatibility fails. Breeding needs long-term space, parent health checks and a plan for every chick, not merely a box.
