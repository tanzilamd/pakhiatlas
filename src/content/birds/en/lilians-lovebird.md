## Meet the bird

A small green lovebird with a pale eye-ring and orange-red face. It is not another name for a peach-faced or Fischer’s lovebird. Natural range: River valleys and woodland habitats in southeastern Africa.

## Recognising the species

An eye-ring alone does not identify the species. Check the scientific identity and provenance; ordinary plumage colour is not a reliable sex test.

## Behaviour and companionship

Allow social observation and slow introductions. A pair is not made by simply placing two birds together; separate birds if chasing or food exclusion develops.

## Housing, movement and cleanup

Provide flight space, multiple feeding stations and safe perches. Avoid crowded mixed cages or larger parrots.

## Planning its diet

Use a balanced lovebird-appropriate feeding plan and introduce suitable vegetables. Favourite seeds should not become the entire diet.

## Pairing and breeding welfare

Before breeding, establish species identity, compatible pairing and health. Repeated clutches or crossing species merely for colour should not be the goal.
