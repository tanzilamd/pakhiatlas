## A change from normal matters

Observe daily appetite, posture, activity, breathing and droppings. Record weight with a suitable scale if this can be done without distress; interpret trends with the veterinarian. Fluffed feathers or quiet behavior alone cannot name a disease.

## Urgent warning signs

Breathing difficulty, collapse, seizures, uncontrolled bleeding, suspected poisoning or major injury require urgent professional attention. Call ahead to confirm the clinic can see birds. Avoid force-feeding, unprescribed medicine and invasive home procedures.

## Transport with less stress

Use a secure, ventilated carrier appropriate to the bird. Avoid unstable high perches for a weak bird. Keep the carrier quiet and protected from temperature extremes without blocking airflow. Bring diet, exposure and symptom history, photographs and prior records. Do not delay urgent travel for perfect paperwork.
