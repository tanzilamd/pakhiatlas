## Morning: look before you clean

Before replacing the lining, look at appetite, droppings, posture and activity. Compare with the individual’s normal pattern rather than another bird online. Check that water is accessible and that a bowl apparently full of seed is not just husks.

## Food and cleaning

Offer the appropriate feeding plan, wash soiled containers and remove spoiled fresh food. Check perches, doors, toys and floor areas for damage or contamination. Keep other household animals away while doors are open.

## Activity without pressure

Provide safe movement and species-appropriate enrichment. During supervised flight, control fans, windows, hot surfaces and other pets. Use short voluntary interaction and allow retreat. A quiet session can still be valuable; constant handling is not the goal.

## Evening and interruptions

Check intake and normal activity before the rest period. Keep sleep predictable and prepare a carrier, clinic contact and alternate carer for emergencies or travel. Leave clear written instructions for anyone providing care, including usual food and signs that should prompt a call.
