## Prevent heat exposure

Provide shade and ventilation without exposure to fumes, predators or escape routes. A cage near a sunlit window can overheat. Panting, weakness or loss of balance require prompt attention; move away from the heat source and contact a veterinarian. Do not plunge a distressed bird into ice water.

## Humidity and spoilage

During humid weather inspect food storage and bedding for dampness and mold. Remove spoiled fresh food and clean water containers frequently. Ventilate safely; a fan must never be accessible to a free-flying bird.

## Have an outage plan

Keep a carrier, clinic contacts, safe lighting and a household care plan ready. Fuel-burning generators must remain away from enclosed bird areas because fumes can be lethal. Avoid unsafe improvised heating and sudden temperature swings.
