## Separate first

Keep a newcomer separate from resident birds, with separate utensils and careful hand hygiene. Shared air can matter for infection risk; agree practical arrangements with the veterinarian. There is no universal quarantine duration that makes every bird safe.

## Reduce pressure

Place food and water where they can be found without climbing unfamiliar equipment. Keep the environment predictable. Observe intake and droppings without repeated handling. Introduce people gradually and let the bird retreat.

## Arrange an assessment

Discuss source, prior contact with other birds, weight, current food and any changes with the clinic. Testing is selected for the individual; do not give preventive antibiotics without a veterinary prescription.
