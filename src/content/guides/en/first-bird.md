## Start with the household

Discuss sound, feather dust, safe flight space, daily cleaning and ongoing costs with everyone at home. Consider cats, dogs, children, cooking fumes and access to windows or fans. A bird is not a low-maintenance decoration.

## Plan before acquiring

Identify a veterinarian willing and equipped to assess birds. Arrange secure housing, a carrier, suitable food, cleaning supplies and a separate space for a new arrival. Ask who will provide daily care during illness, travel or a power outage.

## Choose an individual responsibly

Ask for age, diet, health history, identification and lawful provenance. Watch normal activity from a distance. Do not buy an unweaned chick or take a visibly ill bird home without a veterinary plan. A good appearance does not rule out disease.
