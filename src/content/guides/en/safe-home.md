## Useful space over decoration

Select secure housing with room for movement, appropriate bar spacing and no entrapment points. Arrange perches so droppings do not fall into bowls. Toys should not obstruct the usable flight area. Inspect fasteners and damaged parts daily.

## Control fumes and moving hazards

Keep birds away from cooking fumes, smoke, aerosols and overheated non-stick cookware. Before supervised exercise, switch off fans, close exits and make windows visible. Keep other pets physically separate. Do not rely on a bird to recognise a hot or transparent surface.

## Perches, cleaning and rest

Offer safe perches with varied appropriate diameters and check feet for pressure injuries. Clean soiled surfaces, rinse products thoroughly and allow them to dry before use. Avoid strong fumes. Provide a consistent quiet sleep period without isolating a bird from all daytime activity.
