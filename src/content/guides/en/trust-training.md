## Find a comfortable distance

Start where the bird can watch you without trying to escape. Offer a valued reward for a small voluntary action. Keep sessions brief and stop before distress. Freezing can be fear, not consent. Never chase a bird around a cage to make it tame.

## Step-up and targeting

Reward approaching a stable offered perch before asking for a full step. A target can guide voluntary movement; it must not be used to corner the bird. Adapt to mobility, confidence and species. A bird that refuses may be frightened, tired or unwell.

## Biting and loud calls

Look at what happened before a bite or call: proximity, fear, sleep disruption or a need for contact. Avoid yelling or physical punishment. Sudden behavior change warrants a health check. Give foraging, safe movement and predictable interaction; do not promise silence or speech.
