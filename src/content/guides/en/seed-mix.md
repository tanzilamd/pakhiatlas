## Which birds is this plan for?

Budgies, lovebirds and conures eating seeds does not make this exact ratio appropriate for them. Lorikeets, mynas, finches, pigeons and quail also need different feeding plans. Choose ingredients and proportions for the species and individual. See [cockatiel daily feeding](../cockatiel-feeding/) and [balanced-diet principles](../balanced-diet/) for the wider plan.

## Match quantities to the ratio

Untick ingredients to leave them out. The retained ingredients preserve their original proportions to one another; weights and cost use the new selected total. This does not establish the nutritional suitability of the altered blend.

Choose a target batch and enter its weight to scale the retained ingredients together. One tenth of the original list is 1.07 kg. Enter stock quantities to see shortages and leftovers too.

In stock mode, enter every available ingredient in kilograms, using 0 for a known absence. The first ingredient to run out limits the largest batch in the same ratio. The calculator does not increase other ingredients to fill the missing share.

Enter your own quoted or paid price per kilogram. Used-portion cost, stock value at entered prices and additional buying cost are distinct. A partial cost with missing prices is not a full bill. These are not current Dhaka market rates.

## Duration and bird capacity

Enter your own measured dry mix allocation per bird/day. Edible kernels and the weight of mix taken with husks are different; include normal discarded material in the use that depletes your stock. It is not a universal feeding allowance.

Change bird count and planning days to explore capacity. Stock exhaustion is not a guarantee of food shelf life; feeding needs, actual intake and storage condition still matter.

## Identify, inspect and mix

Check the identity of china, kaun, canary seed and millet. A four-millet blend may already contain some separately listed ingredients. Mixed canary feed is different from the identified canary seed. Do not guess substitutions for ambiguous names.

Inspect each ingredient. Buy food-grade seed untreated for planting; reject dust, pests, webbing, damp odour and mold. Floating or germination does not establish freedom from contamination or toxins.

Use a clean dry vessel and a scale. Weigh the calculated quantities, combine dry and label ingredients, ratio and date. Do not improvise medication or supplements to turn the list into a complete food.

## Storage

Choose small batches that suit actual turnover. Use clean dry covered containers away from heat, steam and pests. Empty and clean before topping up, and keep wet scoops out.

Do not salvage moldy batches by washing, sun-drying or diluting with fresh seed. Never return soaked or sprouted food to dry storage. The actual transition method is maintained in one place: [introducing new food](../food-transition/).
