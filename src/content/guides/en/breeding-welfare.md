## Do not start with a nest box

Before encouraging breeding, assess health, relatedness, compatibility, space, lawful provenance and access to urgent veterinary care. Make a plan for every chick and for pairs that do not get along. Avoid repeated clutches as a production target.

## Know when to call

Straining, weakness, abnormal posture, difficulty breathing or sitting low can be concerning, especially in a laying bird. Seek prompt veterinary assessment. Do not massage the abdomen, manipulate an egg or give a guessed calcium dose at home.

## Parent care and weaning

If chicks are not being fed or are weak, obtain experienced veterinary help promptly. Amateur syringe feeding risks aspiration, burns and other serious harm. Do not provide an unweaned chick to an inexperienced buyer. Weaning must follow individual development rather than a sale deadline. Keep accurate identity and ancestry records.
