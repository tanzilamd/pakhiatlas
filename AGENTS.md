# PakhiAtlas contributor contract

Static Astro 7 + TypeScript + Tailwind 4 + Pagefind. GitHub Pages only. No backend, accounts, analytics, runtime APIs or AI services. Use the current isolated checkout; do not create worktrees unless requested.

## Architecture
- `src/data/birds.json`: canonical taxonomy, aliases, display traits. One slug per species; morphs and domestic breeds are not species.
- `src/content/{birds,guides}/{bn,en}/*.md`: paired localized prose with JSON frontmatter (valid YAML). Content Collections validate records.
- `src/data/references.ts`: reference ledger with actual retrieval status; clinical/legal primary links remain unverified leads, while secondary media/species and archived price records have documented retrieval. Never treat source presence as verified care claims.
- `src/data/wildlife.json` + `src/content/wildlife`: native educational entries, excluded from companion recommendations.
- `src/data/varieties.json`: colour/domestic varieties linked to canonical species.
- `src/data/photos.json` + `PhotoGallery.astro`: real local photos only; use `npm run media:add` after species/provenance review. Follow the owner’s latest preference: all creator/license/source credits on the localized sources/acknowledgements page, linked in the footer; photo captions describe the bird only. Never overlay credits on cards. Preferred cover selection lives in data/media.ts and primary:true metadata; cockatiel cover is lutino. Read docs/MEDIA_SOURCES.md: 56 distinct real photos cover all 43 published species; GFDL assets need full text + received original copy as well as credits. Never restore generic artwork as a species photograph.
- `src/data/foods.json`: qualified food entries, sources and bilingual text.
- `src/data/site.ts`: base-aware URL helper. Every local path must use it.
- `src/pages/[lang]`: static routes; shared-slug language switch. `Layout.astro` provides global controls and metadata.

## Non-negotiable editorial rules
All current articles are editorial drafts because source retrieval was blocked. Preserve useful detail; remove repetition rather than shortening for a word-count target. The owner requests reader-facing editorial presentation without draft labels, network diagnostics or missing-information filler. Keep actual research/access status in docs and metadata. Omit unsupported claims; never label unchecked content as verified or veterinarian-reviewed. Never claim verification without evidence. Read docs/COVERAGE.md and docs/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md first. Keep legal information brief, avoid unverified legal assurances, and do not equate CITES with domestic legality. No wild-caught pet promotion. No diagnosis by droppings colour, medication doses, amateur hand-feeding procedures, invented measurements or veterinarian endorsements. Passage-level source mapping and review remain required before claiming a verified review. No fake or misidentified photos. Keep English/Bangla essential information equivalent and natural, with a stable shared slug. Do not replace Bangla prose with untranslated English.

## Commands and delivery
`npm ci`; `npm run dev`; `npm run check`; `npm run build`; `npm test`. Set ASTRO_TELEMETRY_DISABLED=1 in restricted machines. Scaffolds: `npm run new:bird -- slug`, `npm run new:guide -- slug` (templates intentionally fail validation until written). Check browser behavior at 360, 768 and 1440px. Test language links, local search, tools and keyboard use. Never claim tests you did not execute.
Actions uses configure-pages origin/base outputs and official deploy-pages. Do not change CNAME or custom domains. Verify actual API and deployed URL before claiming deployment. Preserve legitimate repository author identity; no AI coauthor trailers. See docs/CONTENT_GUIDE.md.

## Photo-first revision
PhotoCredit.astro centralises compact attribution. VarietyGuide.astro links real photos to explicitly matched varietyIds; do not guess genetic mutations from a pale individual. PhotoSwipe 5.4.4 replaces the original native dialog; its core loads on demand. The viewer supports zoom, swipe/drag, next/previous and Escape/focus restoration. Main-stage photos and no-JS links remain local. Mobile TOC is collapsed initially so the photo appears sooner. New published species require real photos. Prefer clearly licensed sources. The owner explicitly requested completing source-attributed images without waiting for further license research: six records honestly retain rightsStatus:not-stated, licenseConfirmed:false and Source credit; this is not a reuse grant. Never relabel those CC/public-domain or invent a photographer. Three cockatiel stock files retain collection-level credits, with no invented per-file author mapping. See docs/MEDIA_SOURCES.md.

## Unified explorer and useful tools
`birds/index.astro` lists companion and native records together, with one synchronized group filter, quick choices for the four priority groups, and name/noise/size/interaction filters (no favourites filter). Native records retain wildlife routes and conservation labels and are excluded from companion finder/comparison. Legacy wildlife/varieties index URLs redirect to birds; do not restore duplicate species or morph indexes. Varieties remain ONLY inside their canonical species profile. Featured order prioritises budgie, cockatiel, green-cheeked conure and peach-faced lovebird.

`src/data/care-traits.json` is the canonical bilingual practical comparison vocabulary and per-species trait assignment. No repeated generic cleaning paragraphs in birds.json. Every companion species must have valid traits and a distinct watchOut note; do not invent numeric lifespans or measurements just to fill a table. `src/lib/finder.ts` contains deterministic readiness and preference rules, separated from BirdFinder.astro rendering. Silent-home, missing daily care, unsafe housing and absent care budget produce preparation steps rather than bird suggestions. Dust sensitivity is NOT an allergy-safety assessment.

Tools are independent Astro components behind ordinary hash links: comparison, nine-question finder, food checker and taka budget worksheet. Without JS all educational/static content and photo links are readable. Do not label the budget worksheet as current market prices. Read docs/PRICE_RESEARCH.md: direct Dhaka listing retrieval was blocked. prices.json contains one explicitly qualified, archived secondary Bangladesh budgie range for 2025–2026 per adult bird; it is not a current Dhaka survey. No other market ranges were invented or inferred from demo data. The owner requested Dhaka specifically, with independent research rather than owner-supplied prices.

Feeding: read docs/FEEDING_REVIEW.md. No universal seed recipe/spoon ration, medicinal neem/tulsi schedule, routine insoluble grit for parrots or casual supplement dosages. Seed-mix instructions concern preparation/hygiene, not a nutritionally complete formulation. Cockatiel, seed-mix and Dhaka-budget guides are paired bn/en Markdown. New photographic provenance and rejected wrong-morph examples are recorded in MEDIA_SOURCES.md.

## Feeding and acknowledgements revision

`/bn/feeding/` and `/en/feeding/` are main-navigation hubs: cockatiel plan, seed-mix preparation, balanced diet, four species-food anchors and all 26 food entries by default. Food checker combines free-text name, food group and precaution filters; no canned search tags. Avoid duplicate ingredient entries. All four priority profiles have `#daily-food` and detailed arrival, housing, daily care, interaction, rest and FAQ chapters in both languages. Keep their practical depth and natural Bangla.

All external article references and per-photo creator/license/original/GFDL credits are centralized on `/[lang]/sources/`; `/[lang]/media/` redirects there and is noindex. Source IDs stay in frontmatter and data for traceability; centralizing display never removes the ledger or reuse obligations. Do not restore conversion jargon under photos. Cover selectors are shared through data/media.ts, including variety cards. Budgie uses the blue bird and cockatiel uses the bird-focused lutino on a perch. Bird-card images use contain to preserve bodies and tails. Pearl and whiteface photos are present, with honest source-only rights records. Missing morph photos must not be fabricated or inferred from unrelated species.
