# Research standard

Retrieve original AAV/MSD/VCA/RSPCA material for care, CDC for zoonotic hygiene, taxonomic authorities for species, and official Bangladesh law/CITES for regulations. Record URL, actual title, publication/update date if given, retrieval date, relevant passage or section, supported claim and any uncertainty. Never invent a date or title; current reference names are navigational labels, not bibliographic verification.

Local hobbyists or sellers may establish a report of availability, never legality, breeding provenance or medical correctness. Require dated corroboration and label confidence. Every numeric claim needs a direct traceable source. When sources conflict, describe scope and uncertainty or omit the claim. Species traits are variable, not promises.

Current network-denied attempts are not research completion. Do not promote drafts until the ledger supports every major factual claim and both translations have editorial review. Use qualified avian clinical review when possible, and claim it only with documented consent. Avoid diagnosis, drug doses, home invasive care and amateur hand-feeding instructions. Protect copyright: paraphrase original explanations, use brief quotations only when necessary, license all media.


Secondary datasets require plausibility checks, even when they include citations. In the UX revision the retrieved OrnithoDex data assigned a suspect 15 cm size to cockatiel; it was rejected, not imported or presented as verified. Biological descriptions, visible photo identification and referenced primary pages are separate evidence levels. Do not reuse foreign catalog prices as Dhaka prices, or repository sample records as observed market quotes. Read PRICE_RESEARCH.md.
