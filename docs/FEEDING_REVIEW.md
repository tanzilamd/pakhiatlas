# Review of the owner’s cockatiel note — 2026-11-02

The original note proposed vegetables three days/week, fruit one day, egg or overnight-soaked legumes twice weekly, a large multi-seed batch, 3–4 tablespoons daily for two birds, neem/tulsi twice monthly, grit for digestion, calcium/mineral blocks and a forbidden list that contradicted the spinach ingredient.

Published corrections, in both languages:

- A seed blend is not made nutritionally complete by ingredient count. No universal kilogram ratio or spoon ration is prescribed. Actual intake, husks, body condition and diet transitions matter.
- Suitable vegetable variety is introduced regularly rather than relegating vegetables to three days and leaving seed-only days. Fruit is a limited extra, not the staple.
- Spinach is not both mandatory and universally forbidden: small amounts can be rotated, not treated as the sole green/calcium source.
- Overnight soaking is not equivalent to cooking or sterilising legumes. Wet/warm food hygiene matters; no risky amateur sprouting timetable is provided.
- No compulsory twice-weekly egg ration or automatic breeding/molt supplements are prescribed.
- Cockatiels/budgies/lovebirds/conures generally do not need insoluble grit to digest hulled seed. Soluble calcium sources and stone grit are distinguished. No supplement doses or blanket iron/iodine regimen.
- No routine medicinal neem/tulsi schedule. The food-checker entry concerns the medicinal use and does not falsely declare every use of these plants toxic.
- Remove apple seeds/core and stone-fruit pits; do not claim every seed in every fruit is identically toxic.
- Small, dated, dry batches; identifiable ingredients; no pesticide-treated planting seed; reject mold rather than washing/drying to salvage it.
- Bathing is voluntary, ventilation excludes fumes, and sun exposure requires shade and supervision.

References linked: VCA Cockatiels: Feeding; VCA Birds: General Nutrition; AAV owner resources; CDC hygiene. These original reference URLs were not directly retrieved in the restricted environment. Do not claim passage-level or veterinary review. The original mixture was not republished as an endorsed complete diet.
