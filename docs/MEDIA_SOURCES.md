# Photograph provenance — 2026-11-02

65 distinct real photographs cover every one of the 45 published species (40 companion, 5 native). They produce 180 optimized local WebP variants. All source images were opened and visually inspected; generated art, wrong-species substitutions and watermarked previews were rejected. No source-repository programs were executed. Files were ordinary pre-existing public GitHub assets; no blocked-domain requests were relayed.

## Identification and presentation

Lutino/normal-grey/pied cockatiel photo tags are visual examples, not pedigree or sex guarantees. The pale-headed green-cheeked conure is NOT assigned a guessed pineapple/cinnamon mutation. The domestic pigeon photo shows ancestral rock-dove appearance, not Giribaz or Fantail. The flying lineolated flock is not described as a close-up. Timneh is not substituted with Congo grey. Quail imagery shows domestic mottled plumage without a guessed colour-mutation name. Other pictures in the Jenday source folder included a watermarked stock image, a labelled chick montage and a sun conure: all rejected. Only the matching green-winged Jenday photograph was imported.

Nanday DSC02958 was cropped at left=700, top=350, width=2000, height=2000 from its 3981×2654 original to make the bird visible; no bird anatomy or colour was altered. Other output processing: resizing and WebP conversion.

Credits now live on the single localized sources/acknowledgements page (the latest owner preference). Gallery captions describe only the pictured bird. Bird-card image overlays are deliberately absent per owner preference. PhotoCredit.astro centralises source/creator, applicable stated license, modification notice, and original/GFDL links. PhotoSwipe 5.4.4 now opens profile and variety photos with local zoom, swipe/drag, arrows and keyboard navigation; ordinary full-image links work without JavaScript. The core loads on demand. Its MIT text is retained in public/licenses/PhotoSwipe-MIT.txt. Variety pictures only render where an explicit matching varietyIds association exists.

## UX revision photo selection

Four additional photographs came from pre-existing files in allsiterepos/trg-catalog at 209d16cc3aad13342138f0603847876b09a0454b. catalog.json explicitly credits E dog95 (public domain, cinnamon green-cheek), therouxdown (CC BY-SA 2.0, pineapple green-cheek), Alana Elliott (CC BY 2.0, blue budgie) and Tanya Dropbear (CC BY 2.0, blue ringneck). Images were individually opened. The cinnamon photo gives a substantially clearer body/wing/tail view and is now the primary green-cheek image. Original third-party pages were not retrieved; evidence is the pinned catalog declaration. Source retail prices are foreign and were NOT used as Bangladesh prices.

A catalog file labelled albino ringneck showed a grey-toned bird with a dark neck ring; it was rejected rather than shown as albino. Also avoid tagging every generic assortment photo with the advertised seller mutation: some source records explicitly say colour may vary. Mutation labels are visual examples and never genotype, sex or provenance guarantees.

The 12 new species use real images with explicit per-file README credits in the same pinned OrnithoDex collection, including a CC0 kakariki and public-domain scarlet-chested parrot image. Their biological records were read separately. A suspect 15 cm cockatiel measurement in that secondary dataset was NOT copied into our comparison or profiles. No numerical measurements were imported blindly.
